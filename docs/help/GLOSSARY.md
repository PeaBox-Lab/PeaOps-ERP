
# Glossary

- AR: Accounts Receivable.
- AP: Accounts Payable.
- COGS: Cost of Goods Sold.
- GL: General Ledger.
- RFQ: Request for Quotation.
- PO: Purchase Order.
- Idempotency: safely returning the same result when the same operation is retried.
- Three-way match: comparison of purchase order, receipt and supplier invoice.
- On hand: physically recorded warehouse quantity.
- Reserved: on-hand quantity committed for use.
- Available: on hand minus reserved.
- Natural balance: debit or credit orientation based on account type.
- Posting profile: mapping from business posting roles to GL accounts.
