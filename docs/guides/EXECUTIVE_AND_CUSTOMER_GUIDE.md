
# Executive and Customer Guide

## What PeaOps covers in v0.7

PeaOps provides one cumulative core for Finance, Sales, Procurement, Inventory, cross-module Integration and Insight. It supports Local evaluation, Local Server architecture and a Cloud-ready path, but is not yet a production-GA or compliance-certified release.

## Decisions you own

- Deployment model and data residency.
- Chart of accounts, fiscal calendar and management reporting structure.
- Credit, inventory-negative, matching-tolerance and approval policies.
- Accountable process owners for Quote-to-Cash and Procure-to-Pay.
- Acceptance criteria, cutover timing and production-risk sign-off.

## Governance cadence

Review weekly during implementation: scope, decisions, data quality, defects, controls, training and cutover readiness. After go-live, review financial close, exceptions, ageing, inventory accuracy, supplier performance and integration health.

## Outcome measures

Use v0.7 KPIs for receivable/payable days, quote conversion, integration success and inventory value. Define target values with business owners; the product does not invent targets.
