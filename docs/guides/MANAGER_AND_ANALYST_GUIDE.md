# Manager and Analyst Guide

## Available insight

Income statement, balance sheet, trial balance, ageing, customer sales, supplier commitments, warehouse inventory, integration health, data quality and cross-module KPIs.

## Analysis workflow

1. Confirm period, company, currency and population.
2. Review data-quality findings and statement balance before interpretation.
3. Compare current results with an approved baseline outside v0.7 where needed.
4. Save reusable report parameters.
5. Export CSV and retain the generation context.

## Interpretation cautions

KPIs are derived from available cumulative data. Reporting dimensions, budgets, forecasts, consolidation, cash-flow classification and comparative periods remain future enhancements.
