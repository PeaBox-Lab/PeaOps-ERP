# Procurement User and Buyer Guide

## Workflow

1. Capture and submit a purchase request.
2. Issue an RFQ with a response deadline.
3. Record supplier quotations.
4. Compare total cost and promised date.
5. Convert the selected quotation to a purchase order.
6. Confirm and receive quantities without over-receipt.
7. Submit the supplier invoice for three-way matching.
8. Resolve quantity or price exceptions before automatic journal posting.

The recommendation is transparent and cost/date based; buyers remain accountable for commercial selection and policy compliance.
