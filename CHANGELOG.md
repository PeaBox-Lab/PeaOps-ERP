# Changelog

## 0.5.0 - 2026-11-16

### Added
- Immutable inventory movement ledger.
- Warehouse/item balances and reservations.
- Negative-stock policies and controls.
- Weighted-average valuation foundation.
- Balanced stock transfers.
- Lot and serial tracking foundations.
- Reorder policies and alerts.
- Inventory command-centre interface.
- Inventory rule tests covering quantities, valuation, tracking and replenishment.

### Changed
- Product/API/frontend version advanced to 0.5.0.
- Numbering sequence types expanded for stock transfers.
- Architecture and user documentation expanded for stock control.

## 0.4.0 - 2026-11-16
### Added
- Purchase requests, RFQs, supplier quotes, comparisons, POs, receipts and Procurement command centre.

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-16
### Added
- Sales pricing, quotations, orders, credit controls, AR handoff and Sales command centre.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-16
### Added
- AR/AP, payments, allocations, ageing and Finance command centre.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-16
### Added
- Shared ERP core, GL foundation, audit and deployment branches.
