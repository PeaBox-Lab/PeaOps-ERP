# Community Code of Conduct

PeaOps welcomes respectful participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Be constructive, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, protect private information, credit others, and accept maintainers' moderation decisions. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention, doxxing and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method configured by repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and restrict participation to protect the community.
